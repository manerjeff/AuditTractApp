## 1. Purpose
This policy governs the preparation, independent examination, and public distribution of **[Company Name]**'s SOC 3 General Use Report, providing customers and prospective clients with verified assurance regarding platform security, availability, and confidentiality controls.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all public security claims, trust center materials, CPA auditor engagements, and executive assertions published regarding company systems.

## 3. Core Requirements
1. **Public System Description:** Management shall maintain an unclassified, general-use system description accurately detailing platform architecture, operational boundaries, and security safeguards.
2. **Annual Independent CPA Examination:** An annual SOC 3 examination must be conducted by an independent Certified Public Accountant (CPA) firm under AICPA SSAE 18 standards.
3. **Public Dispensability:** The resulting SOC 3 report and CPA opinion letter may be distributed publicly without non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) to demonstrate operational compliance.
4. **Omission of Confidential Test Details:** Detailed testing procedures, internal control matrices, and specific vulnerability scan outputs included in SOC 2 reports shall be excluded from the public SOC 3 document to protect operational security.
