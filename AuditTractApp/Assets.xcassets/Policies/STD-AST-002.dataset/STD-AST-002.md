## 1. Scope
All employees, contractors, service providers, and temporary personnel operating on Company premises, hosted or outsourced cloud environments, or with access to Company systems must comply with this standard.

## 2. Objectives
Company hardware and software assets must be managed in accordance with information asset protection objectives throughout their entire lifecycle, from procurement to formal disposal. Detailed lifecycle instructions are provided in the System Development Life Cycle Standard.

1. **Production Asset Baselines:** **[Company Name]** will establish and maintain Asset Protection Standards for all system hardware, cloud instances, and virtual infrastructure represented in the production environment.
2. **Configuration Control:** Specific requirements for configuration management and change control are documented in the Configuration Management Standard and Change Control Standard.
3. **Authorized Updates:** All systems, networks, and applications used in production or hosted virtual premises must follow documented change control procedures to ensure only authorized updates are deployed.

## 3. Responsibilities
The **[Data Security Officer]** is the approval authority for the Asset Management Standard.

* **Legal Counsel:** Ensures contracts, vendor agreements, and software licenses enforce asset management standards.
* **Company Management:** Ensures this standard is communicated and understood within respective organizational units.
* **Users & Custodians:** Responsible for maintaining accurate inventory records and adhering to lifecycle management procedures.

## 4. Enforcement and Exception Handling
Failure to comply can result in disciplinary action up to termination of employment or contracts. Requests for exceptions should be submitted to the **[Data Security Officer]**.

## 5. Review and Revision
Reviewed annually in accordance with the Information Security Program Charter.
