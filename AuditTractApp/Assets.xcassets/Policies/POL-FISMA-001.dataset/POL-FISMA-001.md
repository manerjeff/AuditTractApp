## 1. Purpose
This policy establishes mandatory operational procedures to achieve and maintain FISMA compliance and secure federal information systems using the NIST Risk Management Framework (RMF).

## 2. Scope
Applies to all information systems, cloud environments, federal system interconnections, contractors, and agency personnel operating systems on behalf of the federal government.

## 3. RMF Life Cycle Mandates
1. **FIPS 199 Categorization:** Every federal system must undergo formal security categorization (Low, Moderate, High) across Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability.
2. **System Security Plan (SSP):** A comprehensive SSP must document all implemented NIST SP 800-53 controls, system boundaries, and operational dependencies.
3. **Independent Assessment & ATO:** Systems must undergo independent security assessments (SAR) to obtain a formal Authorization to Operate (ATO) signed by the Authorizing Official (AO).
4. **POA&M Management:** All identified control weaknesses must be logged in an active Plan of Action and Milestones (POA&M) tracker and remediated according to federal timelines.
5. **Continuous Monitoring (ConMon):** Monthly vulnerability scans and continuous log monitoring must be executed to maintain operational authorization.
