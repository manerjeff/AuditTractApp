## 1. Scope
All employees, contractors, part-time and temporary workers, and third parties accessing Company systems, premises, or hosted infrastructure are covered by this policy and must comply with associated standards.

## 2. Core Protection Objectives
1. **Access Control:** Access authorization is based on data classification and limited to approved business needs. Proper identification and authentication are enforced per the Access Control Standard.
2. **Remote Access:** Granted only for authorized business needs using Company-approved encrypted tools per the Remote Access Standard.
3. **Physical Protection:** Physical security controls must be applied to areas containing assets or processing facilities per the Physical Access Standard.
4. **Supporting Infrastructure:** Power, network lines, cooling, and hardware utilities must be protected from disruption and periodically inspected.
5. **Encryption:** Restricted and Confidential assets transmitted over non-secure or public networks must be encrypted using Company-approved ciphers per the Encryption Standard.
6. **Data Integrity:** Information assets must be maintained with controls ensuring data is correct, auditable, and reproducible per the Integrity Protection Standard.
7. **Availability & Backups:** Continuity planning, replication, and backup routines must ensure consistent asset availability per the Availability Protection Standard.
8. **Malware Safeguards:** Approved anti-virus software must be active on all endpoints per the Anti-Virus Standard.
9. **Auditing:** Security event auditing must be active and logs securely retained per the Auditing Standard.
10. **Patch Management:** Supporting software infrastructure must be kept up to date with approved vendor patches per the Patch Management Standard.

## 3. Responsibilities
* **Data Security Officer:** Approves and maintains the Asset Protection Standard.
* **Asset Owners:** Authorize user access, define operational procedures, and revoke unnecessary access.
* **Asset Custodians:** Provide secure processing environments and implement procedural safeguards.
* **Users:** Maintain confidentiality, integrity, and availability of assets while under their control.

## 4. Enforcement & Review
Violations may result in disciplinary action up to termination. Exceptions require written sign-off from the **[Data Security Officer]**. Reviewed annually.
