## 1. Purpose
This policy establishes executive security governance and operational requirements to align **[Company Name]**'s technology infrastructure with the HITRUST Common Security Framework (CSF v11).

## 2. Scope
Applies to all systems, cloud infrastructure, endpoints, databases, employees, and third-party contractors accessing, storing, or transmitting Protected Health Information (PHI) or sensitive customer data.

## 3. Mandatory HITRUST Directives
1. **Access Control & MFA:** Logical access to sensitive data repositories requires individual user identification and mandatory multi-factor authentication (MFA).
2. **Encryption:** All electronic ePHI at rest must be encrypted using AES-256. All data in transit must enforce TLS 1.3 encryption.
3. **Vulnerability & Patch SLAs:** Vulnerability scans must be performed monthly, and critical patch updates must be applied within 30 days of release.
4. **Endpoint Compliance:** Mobile devices and laptops accessing company resources must be enrolled in MDM with remote wipe capabilities and disk encryption enabled.
