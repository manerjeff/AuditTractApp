## 1. Purpose
This policy governs the collection, storage, processing, and disclosure of student **Education Records** and **Personally Identifiable Information (PII)** maintained by **[Company Name]** in accordance with the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA).

## 2. Scope
Applies to all employees, contractors, EdTech platform developers, system administrators, and third-party vendors handling student records.

## 3. Mandatory Safeguards
1. **Consent & Disclosure Controls:** Education records shall not be disclosed without prior written consent from parents or eligible students, unless operating under statutory exceptions (e.g., School Official exception).
2. **School Official Designation:** Third-party cloud and software vendors accessing student data must execute Data Protection Addendums (DPAs) designating them as School Officials under direct institutional control.
3. **Data Minimization & Profile Building:** Student data must be used strictly for legitimate educational purposes. Commercial profiling, targeted advertising, or selling student PII is strictly prohibited.
4. **Technical Access Restrictions:** Systems storing student data must enforce Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), multi-factor authentication (MFA), and full-disk encryption (AES-256) at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit.
5. **Auditing & Disclosure Logs:** All accesses, disclosures, and re-disclosures of student education records must be recorded in centralized, write-protected audit logs.
