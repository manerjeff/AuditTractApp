## 1. Purpose
This policy sets forth mandatory standards governing the lawful processing, storage, transfer, and deletion of personal data belonging to users, customers, and employees across **[Company Name]** platforms.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all systems, cloud environments, third-party processors, software engineers, and administrative staff handling Personal Identifiable Information (PII) or personal data.

## 3. Mandatory Safeguards
1. **Lawful Basis & Consent:** Personal data shall be collected and processed strictly under explicit statutory grounds or verifiable user consent.
2. **Data Subject Rights (DSAR):** Data subjects retain the right to inspect, correct, export, and request deletion (Right to be Forgotten) of personal records within 30 days of request.
3. **72-Hour Breach Notification:** Confirmed personal data breaches must be reported to applicable Supervisory Authorities and affected data subjects within 72 hours.
4. **Cross-Border Transfer Safeguards:** International transfers of personal data must utilize Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or verified adequacy frameworks.
