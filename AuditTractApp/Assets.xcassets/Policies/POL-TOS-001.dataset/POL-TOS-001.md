## 1. Scope & Contract Basis
These Customer Terms of Service govern access to and use of **[Company Name]** software platforms, online applications, and platform tools. Entering into an order form or accessing services forms a binding contract between **[Company Name]** and the Customer organization.

## 2. Shared Responsibility Model
Security and compliance is a shared responsibility between **[Company Name]** and the Customer:
* **[Company Name] Responsibilities:** Provisioning application availability, maintaining administrative/physical/technical cloud safeguards, protecting customer data against unauthorized employee access, and providing uptime reliability.
* **Customer Responsibilities:** Managing user account provisioning, securing local endpoint hardware, defining internal authorization permissions, and ensuring lawful collection and transmission of uploaded data.

## 3. Customer Data & Privacy Safeguards
1. **Ownership:** Customer retains sole ownership of all uploaded Customer Data. **[Company Name]** is granted a limited license to process data strictly to maintain, secure, and deliver services.
2. **Data Protections:** **[Company Name]** maintains administrative, physical, and technical safeguards at least as protective as published policies.
3. **Data Portability & Deletion:** Customers may export data during active subscription terms. Following contract termination, **[Company Name]** maintains no ongoing obligation to retain customer data and will execute automated data purges per retention schedules.

## 4. Ethics & Misconduct
Customers and authorized users must comply with Acceptable Use Policies and report perceived ethical or security concerns to `[ethics@company.com]` or `[sirt@company.com]`.
