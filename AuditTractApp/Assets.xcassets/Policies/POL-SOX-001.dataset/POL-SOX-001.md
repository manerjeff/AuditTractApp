## 1. Purpose
This policy defines the governance and testing requirements for IT General Controls (ITGC) supporting applications, databases, and infrastructure involved in financial reporting and internal controls over financial reporting (ICFR).

## 2. Scope
Applies to all ERP systems, accounting software, databases, network infrastructure, and personnel involved in financial data generation or processing.

## 3. Core ITGC Control Domains
1. **Access to Programs and Data:** User access to financial systems must be formally requested and authorized by business owners. Access rights are recertified quarterly, and terminated user access is revoked within 24 hours.
2. **Change Management:** Software updates impacting financial reporting must undergo User Acceptance Testing (UAT) and formal approval before production release. Developers are prohibited from having production deployment privileges.
3. **Computer Operations:** Automated batch processing jobs and financial data transfers are monitored daily. System backups are performed daily and tested periodically to ensure financial data integrity.
