**Framework Alignment:** ISO/IEC 27001 (A.5.14), SOC 2 (CC6.6), NIST SP 800-53 (AC-4, PS-6)

## 1. Scope
All personnel granted access to **[Company Name]** Electronic Mail Resources must comply with this standard.

## 2. Requirements

### A. Business Use & Confidentiality Disclaimers
1. Email resources are provided primarily for official business purposes.
2. All outbound external email transmissions must include the standard corporate confidentiality notice:
   > *"This e-mail message (and any attachment) is intended for the use of the individual or entity to which it is addressed. This message contains information from [Company Name] that may be privileged, confidential, or exempt from disclosure under applicable law. If you are not the intended recipient, any dissemination, distribution, or copying is strictly prohibited. If received in error, please notify the sender immediately and delete all copies."*

### B. Prohibited Practices
1. Personnel shall not use email to forward chain letters, virus hoaxes, or commercial solicitations.
2. Transmission of objectionable, harassing, or sexually explicit content is strictly forbidden.
3. Automated email forwarding rules to non-company or personal email accounts are prohibited.
4. Tools or features that mask or obscure sender identity (e.g., anonymous remailers) are forbidden.

### C. Technical Restrictions & Attachments
1. Email attachments must not exceed **100 MB**. Files exceeding this limit must be transmitted via secure, encrypted portal links.
2. All incoming and outgoing attachments are automatically scanned for malware and viruses.

### D. Right to Monitor
All emails created, received, or stored on corporate servers are Company property. The Company reserves the right to monitor and audit email traffic at any time without prior notice.

## 3. Enforcement & Review
Violations may lead to disciplinary action up to termination. Actual or suspected email misuse must be reported immediately per the Misuse Reporting Standard. Reviewed annually.
