## 1. Scope
All employees, contractors, part-time and temporary workers, and service providers operating on Company premises, hosted/outsourced sites, or who have been granted access to Company information or systems must comply with this standard.

An **information asset** is defined as any data, subset of data, or aggregate of data that has value to the organization or its clients. This includes all electronic media and physical records used by the Company or in support of Company business processes.

## 2. Objectives
The Company defines information classifications based upon sensitivity, criticality, confidentiality/privacy requirements, and value. All information assets must be categorized into one of these four classifications:
1. **Restricted:** Highly sensitive operational, client, or personal data. Requires strict access controls and mandatory encryption at all times.
2. **Confidential:** Sensitive business, technical, or client data. Requires access restrictions and encryption where feasible.
3. **Internal Use:** Information intended for general internal distribution among authorized personnel.
4. **Public:** Information explicitly approved by the Data Security Officer for public distribution.

When information of various classifications is combined, the resulting collection must be classified at the most restrictive level among its sources. Specific guidelines for labeling and handling are detailed in the Information Classification Standard and Information Labeling Standard.
