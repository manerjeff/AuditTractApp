## 1. Purpose & Scope
To manage security risks associated with third-party vendors, cloud infrastructure providers, subservice organizations, and consultants who store, process, or transmit **[Company Name]** data or support critical systems.

## 2. Requirements

### A. Pre-Contract Risk Assessment
1. Prior to contract execution, all prospective vendors must undergo a security risk evaluation proportional to data access level.
2. Vendors handling **Confidential** or **Restricted** data must provide annual independent security certifications (e.g., SOC 1 Type II, SOC 2 Type II, or ISO 27001 audit assurance reports).
3. Findings from vendor reviews must be cataloged in the Enterprise Risk Register.

### B. Contractual Security Addendums
All vendor contracts and Master Services Agreements (MSAs) must incorporate mandatory Data Protection Addendums (DPAs) stipulating:
* Mandatory incident/breach notification to **[Company Name]** within **twenty-four (24) hours** of discovery.
* Adherence to formal data encryption and access control standards.
* Complete data return or sanitization upon contract termination, with written destruction certification.
* Right-to-audit clauses allowing **[Company Name]** or designated representatives to evaluate security compliance.

### C. Annual Review
Vendor SOC reports, bridge letters, and Complementary User Entity Controls (CUECs) must be reviewed annually by Information Security to ensure ongoing compliance.

## 3. Enforcement & Review
Failure to follow vendor risk evaluation procedures may result in contract termination or disciplinary action. Reviewed annually.
