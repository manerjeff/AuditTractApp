**Document ID:** STD-BYOD-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** [Effective Date]  
**Approved By:** [Data Security Officer / Executive Management]  
**Framework Alignment:** ISO/IEC 27001 (A.6.7, A.8.1), SOC 2 (CC6.1, CC6.6), NIST SP 800-53 (AC-19)

---

## 1. Scope
All employees, contractors, and temporary workers granted permission to access Company Technology Resources via personal smartphones, tablets, or laptops must comply with this standard.

* **Jail-Breaking / Rooting:** Refers to disabling control restrictions built into mobile hardware/OS to gain elevated control.
* **Technology Resources:** Refers to systems, networks, equipment, software, and cloud applications used to store, transmit, or process Company data.

## 2. Requirements

### A. Business & Personal Use Rules
1. BYOD access is provided primarily for official business purposes in support of company goals.
2. Limited personal use is acceptable provided it does not interfere with operations or reflect adversely on **[Company Name]**.
3. Users must accept all corporate BYOD security software, containerization, and management profiles before accessing company data.
4. Expense reimbursement policies for devices or service plans (if applicable) are determined by executive management.

### B. Improper Use & Prohibitions
1. BYOD access must not violate any local, state, or federal laws.
2. Jail-breaking or rooting personally owned devices accessing company data is **strictly prohibited**. Disabling security profiles will result in immediate revocation of access and potential termination.
3. Users must not use personal email or unapproved third-party file storage accounts (e.g., personal DropBox, Google Drive, or personal iCloud) to store or transmit company data.
4. Users must adhere to federal and state driving safety laws (e.g., no texting while driving).

### C. Security & Remote Wipe
1. All BYOD storage containing company files must enforce strong full-disk encryption.
2. Sensitivity data including PII, credit card records, and customer databases must remain protected inside approved containers.
3. **Right to Wipe:** Lost or stolen devices will be locked and remotely wiped of corporate data immediately. The Company assumes no responsibility for lost personal files or photos and advises users to maintain independent personal backups.
4. Upon employee resignation or termination, BYOD hardware must be purged of all corporate directories, emails, application data, and client files.

### D. Privacy Expectations
Users should have no expectation of privacy regarding corporate data created, received, or transmitted over Company Technology Resources. The Company reserves the right to restrict access based on geolocation and enforce end-to-end encryption.

## 3. Responsibilities & Reporting
The **[Data Security Officer]** approves and enforces the BYOD Standard. Users must report a lost or stolen BYOD device containing company data to IT/Security within **2 hours** of realizing the device is missing.

## 4. Enforcement & Review
Violations will result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment or contracts. Reviewed annually.
