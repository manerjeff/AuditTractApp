## 1. Scope & Objective
The Change Advisory Board (CAB) is established to communicate, coordinate, and certify all changes deemed necessary by the Data Security Officer that are scheduled to be implemented into the Information Technology (IT) production environment within **[Company Name]**.

The CAB provides a forum where representatives from all required areas are made aware of changes that could directly or inadvertently impact their respective environments. Open discussion of changes prior to deployment allows IT to remain proactive in deployment preparation. The CAB also aids in compliance with fundamental audit practices regarding the review and approval of changes prior to production release.

## 2. CAB Governance & Agenda
The CAB shall meet at least weekly and will be facilitated by an Information Security representative.

### A. Membership & Voting Authority
The CAB is composed of representatives from Senior Development, Client Services, and Support Management, with tie-breaker authority held by the Data Security Officer:
* **Data Security Officer (Board Chair)\*** *(Tie-breaker authority)*
* **Development Director or Designee\***
* **Client Services / Support Management or Designee\***
*\* Denotes Voting Members*

### B. Voting & Documentation
* A majority vote of voting members in attendance is required to approve any business presented to the CAB.
* Any CAB member may request additional testing, training, or evaluation prior to approval, with agreed-upon completion dates.
* Minutes and decision logs recording CAB approvals shall be permanently archived within the designated ticketing system alongside the deployment task for historical reference and audit evidence.

### C. Agenda Structure
1. **Outstanding Requests:** Review requests in Pending Certification waiting on approval.
2. **Emergency Requests:** Reviewed individually. Emergency changes require retrospective explanation and approval documentation.
3. **Regular Requests:** Page-by-page ticket review and voting.

## 3. Responsibilities
The **[Data Security Officer]** is the approval authority for the Change Advisory Board Charter. Company management is responsible for ensuring that change control standards are communicated and followed within their respective teams.

## 4. Enforcement & Review
Failure to follow change control procedures may result in disciplinary action up to termination of employment or contracts. Reviewed annually in accordance with the Information Security Program Charter.
