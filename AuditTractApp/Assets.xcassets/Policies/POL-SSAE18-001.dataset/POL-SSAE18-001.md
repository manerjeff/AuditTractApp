## 1. Purpose
This policy governs the execution and oversight of independent Service Organization Control (SOC 1, SOC 2, and SOC 3) examinations conducted in accordance with Statement on Standards for Attestation Engagements No. 18 (SSAE 18).

## 2. Scope
Applies to all cloud services, hosting platforms, data processing systems, and third-party vendor relationships supporting customer service delivery.

## 3. Core Attestation Requirements
1. **Management Assertion:** Executive management must prepare and execute a formal written assertion attesting to the fair presentation of the system description and operational effectiveness of internal controls.
2. **Complementary User Entity Controls (CUECs):** System descriptions must explicitly define CUECs required for client implementation to achieve security objectives.
3. **Subservice Organization Governance:** Management must review SOC reports and bridge letters from critical subservice vendors, evaluating Complementary Subservice Organization Controls (CSOCs).
4. **Audit Period Continuity:** Evidence collection must cover the full Type II examination period without temporal gaps in audit log retention or sample evidence.
