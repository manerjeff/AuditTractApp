## 1. Scope
This policy applies to all personnel managing, processing, scanning, or fulfilling client document retrieval requests within **[Company Name]** scanning facilities or cloud repositories.

## 2. Requirements

### A. Physical Barcoding & Labeling
1. Physical document batches received for scanning must be logged into the inventory tracking system and assigned a unique barcode identifier (`*<4D Client ID><4N Box #>*`).
2. Physical boxes and workstations must be labeled according to confidentiality classification:
   * **Restricted Packages:** Marked with **Red Labels**.
   * **Confidential Packages:** Marked with **Orange Labels**.
