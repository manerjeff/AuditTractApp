## 1. Corporate Character & Values
Our relationships, goals, and interactions must reflect our commitment to corporate integrity and lawfulness.

* **Integrity & Honesty:** Striving for excellence and doing the right thing even when no one is watching.
* **Openness & Respect:** Maintaining a professional environment free from intimidation, intolerance, or discrimination.
* **Accountability:** Honoring commitments, taking responsibility for actions, and consistently seeking opportunities to improve quality.

## 2. Mandatory Conduct Guidelines
1. **Compliance with Laws:** Personnel must comply with all applicable local, state, and federal laws and regulations governing company operations.
2. **Anti-Corruption & Gifts:** **[Company Name]** strictly prohibits bribery, kickbacks, or corrupt payments to public officials or private entities. Personnel must not accept gifts, hospitality, or favors that could compromise business objectivity.
3. **Confidentiality:** Personnel must safeguard confidential and proprietary non-public information during and after their employment.
4. **Political Activities:** Personal political participation must occur on personal time using personal funds. Corporate resources must not be used for political contributions.

## 3. Reporting Ethical Violations
Personnel are expected to report suspected violations of law, ethics, or security policy to `[ethics@company.com]`. Reports will be investigated promptly, fairly, and confidentially without fear of retaliation.

## 4. Enforcement
Failure to read or sign this Code does not excuse personnel from compliance. Non-compliance will result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment.
